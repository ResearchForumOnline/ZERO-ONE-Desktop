TRUTHFUL SECURITY BOUNDARY

Research stays private. Protection stays proven.

The public client uses established TLS and operating-system cryptography. Experimental ZMath cipher research is not embedded or claimed as active encryption. Disk encryption remains optional and controlled by Windows, including recovery-key handling.

Performance: initial encryption can take time. Modern hardware usually has modest overhead; older or storage-heavy systems may notice more.

APACHE-2.0

Open where trust demands it.
Private where research demands it.

The desktop shell, security contracts, tests and build configuration are open source. Credentials, signing keys, private infrastructure, unpublished datasets and experimental ZMath implementations are not part of the repository or release artifacts.
